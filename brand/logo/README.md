# Logos

The Launch Title mark is the letters LT on a trigger-shaped button: one large shoulder corner and three tight corners.

- `lt-mark.svg` is the default. Use it on any dark surface.
- `lt-mark-mono-light.svg` and `lt-mark-mono-dark.svg` are for one-colour printing and for controller prompts.
- `lt-lockup-horizontal-on-dark.svg` and `lt-lockup-horizontal-on-light.svg` pair the mark with the wordmark on one line.
- `lt-lockup-stacked-on-dark.svg` is for the boot screen and posters.
- `lt-app-icon.svg` is the mark on a navy tile. `lt-x-avatar.svg` is sized for a round avatar crop.

Keep 8 units of clear space around the mark on its 96-unit grid. Do not go below 16 pixels. Never recolour the mark outside these files, stretch it, or set the letters in a font.
