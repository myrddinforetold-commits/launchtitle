# Launch Title

Launch Title is a console-style home for AI-made web games. Anyone tags a game on X, the creator confirms, the game is hosted here, and it plays inside one place that feels like a console. Creators keep 80% of what their game earns.

The tagline is: Every game deserves a launch.

## Voice

Write like a console's system messages: short, declarative, confident.

- Say what happened. "Added. Waiting on @creator." "Live: launchtitle.gg/lantern-bay."
- No hype and no emoji.
- Never say "AI". Every game here is AI-made, and the word is a stigma elsewhere. Talk about games and creators.
- Money is stated plainly. "Creators keep 80% of what their game earns."

## Vocabulary

| Thing | Name |
| --- | --- |
| A curated store game | A Launch Title |

| What a creator is paid | Royalties |
| The uniform art frame for a game | A title card |

## The mark

The mark is the letters LT on a trigger-shaped button. LT is the left trigger on a controller, so the mark is also a control: pulling LT opens the system menu and starts the console.

The files and their rules are in Logos.

## Colour

The interface is navy, never black. Surfaces step up from `void` to `navy`, `deck`, `panel` and `raised`. Text is `paper`; secondary text is `mist`.

Each section owns one colour, and it is the only strong colour on screen in that section:

- Store is `ignition`.
- Library is `library`.
- Creators is `creators`.

`ignition` is also the brand colour, so the mark stays orange in every section. Text on `ignition` and on `creators` is `navy`. For small coloured text on dark surfaces use `ignition-soft` and `creators-soft`.

`online` and `caution` mean a state and nothing else.

## Glass, and where it stops

The app is a window floating over a full-bleed image.

- Navigation is glass: the `glass` token over a 30px backdrop blur, with a `line` border.
- Content is opaque: `deck`, with `panel` cards. Artwork must never show through text.
- The window carries `shadow-window` and a soft glow in the section colour.

## Glow

Glow marks the one thing to press. A primary Play button or a number badge may carry `glow-ignition`. Use one glow per view.

## Type

One family, Urbanist. Titles and numbers are ExtraBold. Page and section headings are SemiBold. Reading text is Medium.

Labels are 11px, bold, uppercase, with wide letter spacing. Use them for tags, filters and small headings only.

## Shape

- The window is `radius-xl`. Cards and the hero are `radius-lg`. Buttons are `radius-md`. Chips are `radius-sm`. Tags and filters are `radius-pill`.
- The store hero cuts its artwork on a diagonal, with a thin `ignition` stripe along the cut. The share card uses the same cut.

## Artwork

Game art fills its frame edge to edge. A title card adds only three things over it: the number chip at top left, the name on a dark strip, and the creator's handle.

Backdrops behind the window are calm, dark images: ink in water for the Store, a blue-hour lake for the Library, a thin orange horizon for the boot screen.

## Icons

Line icons on a 24-unit grid with a 2-unit stroke and round caps and joins. Icons take the text colour, or the section colour when active.

## Not in this version

Components are designed on the Launch Title design canvas but are not yet built as code in this system.
